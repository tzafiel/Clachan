Fàilte dhan Tìr Shàmhach

Walk the Glens at Your Own Pace

Far out in the North Atlantic mist lies a continent larger than Scotland—unmapped, untouched by the frantic machinery that collapsed across the sea, and governed by weather, tides, and honest craft.

Step into your great-plaid as a Way-Warden (Fear-Faire / Bean-Faire). Track original endemic wildlife through wet pine moss, pitch camp anywhere beneath the stars, sail open water, carve Sia-Clachan hex tiles for your sporran, and weave real-life friends' homesteads directly into your world.

Built Different

  • 1:1 Real-Time World: Local-time skies, shifting storms, and mountain snowmelt that feeds every burn, loch, and sea current.
  • Paired Gaelic & English: Authentic Gàidhlig dialogue paired cleanly with English underneath.
  • Zero Hand-Holding: No floating boss bars, no checklist counters, and no forced tutorials. Discovery stays earned.
  • Physical Bird-Post: Send written notes and keepsakes to friends' bothies via your Seabhag-Fàire falcon.
